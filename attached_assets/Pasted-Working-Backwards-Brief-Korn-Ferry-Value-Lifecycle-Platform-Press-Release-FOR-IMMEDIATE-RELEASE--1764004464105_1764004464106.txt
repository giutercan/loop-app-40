The Challenge: Proving Consulting Value Has Been Too Hard

Management consultants face a persistent challenge: demonstrating tangible business value. Traditional approaches involve scattered data across emails, spreadsheets, and notes; manual value case creation that takes weeks; generic industry benchmarks that don't reflect client reality; and no systematic way to track whether promised improvements actually materialized.

The Solution: Three Connected Phases, One Continuous Value Story

The Value Lifecycle Platform introduces three integrated phases:

Discovery Phase – AI automatically analyzes company research, client conversations, and uploaded documents to identify strategic priorities. Instead of consultants spending days synthesizing insights, GPT-4o surfaces the most impactful "jobs to be done" and recommends relevant KPIs with Korn Ferry benchmark data. Consultants can collaborate with clients through secure questionnaires, with AI extracting key metrics and themes from responses.

Alignment Phase – Consultants and clients work together to set baselines and targets for selected KPIs. The platform calculates financial impact automatically, generates stakeholder-specific value narratives using proven Korn Ferry success stories, and creates shareable alignment views that clients can edit directly—no login required. Re-prioritization features allow dynamic adjustment as client needs evolve.

Realization Phase – Track actual KPI progress against agreed baselines and targets. Link business reviews to success stories. Demonstrate concrete value delivery with real numbers, not promises.

What Makes This Different

AI that understands consulting context: Not generic AI—trained on Korn Ferry's methodologies, solution areas, and verified success stories

Frequently Asked Questions
Q: Who is this for?

A: Korn Ferry management consultants working on client engagements where demonstrating measurable business value is critical. Particularly valuable for strategic transformation, organizational effectiveness, and capability-building engagements.

Q: How is this different from CRM or project management tools?

A: CRMs track relationships and activities. Project management tools track tasks and timelines. The Value Lifecycle Platform tracks business value—the actual measurable outcomes that matter to clients. It's purpose-built for the consulting value creation process, not adapted from generic software.

Q: What if clients don't have baseline data for KPIs?

A: The platform includes AI-powered industry baseline generation. For any KPI, consultants can request AI to generate a defensible industry-standard baseline with sources. Clients can also contribute data through simple questionnaires, and the platform attributes all data sources for credibility.

Q: Can clients access the platform?

A: Clients receive secure, shareable links to view and edit KPI baselines/targets without creating accounts. This reduces friction and increases collaboration. All client contributions are attributed by name for transparency.

Q: What happens if priorities change mid-engagement?

A: The platform supports re-prioritization in the Alignment phase. Consultants can adjust which "jobs" are prioritized while maintaining data integrity. The system locks structural changes after Discovery finalization but allows continuous refinement of KPI values as better data emerges.

Q: How does AI ensure recommendations are relevant to Korn Ferry's approach?

A: The AI is integrated with Korn Ferry's knowledge base—solution areas, capability frameworks, and a verified library of success stories. Recommendations align with proven Korn Ferry methodologies, not generic consulting advice.

Q: What if I prefer to work in spreadsheets?

A: You can export data, but the platform eliminates most spreadsheet work through intelligent automation. Consultants report the AI does in minutes what previously took them days in Excel.

Q: How do I prove the value was actually delivered?

A: The Realization phase tracks actual KPI performance against baselines and targets. You can link business reviews to specific KPIs and success stories, creating an evidence-based narrative of delivered value with real numbers.

Q: Is my client data secure?

A: Yes. The platform includes secure authentication, encrypted shareable tokens for client access, and comprehensive audit trails showing who contributed what data and when.

Q: What's the learning curve?

A: Consultants familiar with value-based selling pick it up in one engagement. The platform guides you through each phase with clear workflows and AI assistance at every step.
