# Korn Ferry Value Lifecycle Platform
## Executive & Strategic Summary

---

## What We Built

The **Korn Ferry Value Lifecycle Platform** is an end-to-end client engagement management system that transforms how consultants, sales teams, and delivery professionals track, communicate, and realize value throughout the client journey. 

It's a unified workspace that guides users through a structured **4-stage sales journey** (Discover → Design Outcomes → Client Alignment → Handoff) and continues into **Delivery execution** with health tracking, business reviews, and success capture.

### Core Capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **AI-Powered Discovery** | GPT-4o generates strategic insights, industry context, and methodology-tagged questions for client conversations |
| **Guided Sales Methodology** | Integrates SPIN Selling, Miller Heiman Strategic Selling, and Professional Selling Skills frameworks |
| **Outcome Journey Design** | Structured implementation paths with phases, quick wins, and milestones for each value outcome |
| **Client Collaboration Portal** | Secure, shareable links for customers to review and confirm value commitments |
| **Seamless Handoff** | Bundles confirmed outcomes with full delivery roadmaps for CSM/Delivery teams |
| **Value Realization Tracking** | KPI health dashboards, QBR support, and success story capture |

## Why It Matters: The Strategic Problem

### The Challenge Before
1. **Disconnected Handoffs**: Sales promises weren't clearly translated to Delivery, leading to misaligned expectations
2. **Inconsistent Methodology**: Different consultants applied Korn Ferry IP differently, affecting quality
3. **Value Leakage**: Without systematic tracking, proving ROI to clients was difficult
4. **Time-Intensive Research**: Consultants spent hours on manual company research before every engagement
5. **Siloed Information**: Client insights, commitments, and progress lived in spreadsheets and emails

### The Solution
A unified platform that embeds Korn Ferry's proven frameworks into every step, automates research, structures handoffs, and tracks value realization with full provenance.
