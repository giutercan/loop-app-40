1. ASSESS (Success Profiles, Standardised Assessments)

These offerings create the data spine for hiring, promotion and development.

Success Profiles & Role Design

Jobs we do
Define what “good” looks like for each role, align roles to strategy, anchor selection/promotion, support workforce planning, feed digital profiles.

Primary KPI
Quality of Hire at 6 months (QoH6) — composite index (0–100) combining objective performance (50%), manager rating (30%) and retention (20%).

Supporting KPIs
Time to Productivity (days); Hiring Manager Satisfaction (1–5).

Measurement & sources
Baseline = previous cohort mean over prior 12 months if available; measure at 6 months; sources: HRIS, performance systems, manager surveys. Use matched cohort design if possible.

Translation note
Financial impact = (QoH6_delta) × (# hires per year) × (avg margin per role) × realisation rate. Show arithmetic in app.

Standardised Assessments & Assessments at Scale

Jobs we do
Objectively measure capability, scale delivery, provide norms, integrate into workflows, ensure Responsible AI.

Primary KPI
Predictive Validity (AUC or correlation r) — the statistical link between assessment score and job performance/retention.

Supporting KPIs
Assessment Completion Rate (%); Time to Offer (days).

Measurement & sources
Historic or prospective validation with holdout sets; sources: assessment platform, HRIS, performance ratings; produce confidence intervals and bias checks.

Translation note
Model improvements reduce mis-hires: compute avoided replacement cost and incremental productivity minus assessment costs. Include fairness tests and Responsible AI result in appendix.

2. DEVELOP (Leadership & Development including AI-Ready Leader)

Builds leaders, embeds behaviour, links learning to business outcomes, prepares leaders for Human+AI.

Leadership & Development Journeys

Jobs we do
Build leaders who deliver outcomes, change behaviour, link learning to business KPIs, develop succession, measure programmes.

Primary KPI
Business KPI Delta attributable to participants (e.g., revenue per team % change, productivity per FTE change).

Supporting KPIs
Competency Gain (assessment/360 delta); Behavioural Application Rate (% applying skill on job).

Measurement & sources
Kirkpatrick Level 4+ designs: matched cohorts or staged rollouts; baseline 12 months preferred; sources: finance systems, operational KPIs, 360 and coaching logs.

Translation note
Value = KPI_delta × coverage (teams/users) × unit financial value × realisation rate. Ensure attribution model documented.

AI-Ready Leader (capability inside L&D)

Jobs we do
Prepare leaders for Human+AI decisions, build AI literacy, set governance, increase speed and quality of data-driven decisions.

Primary KPI
AI Readiness Index (0–100) or Decision Lead Time (hours) for representative decisions.

Supporting KPIs
AI Adoption Rate (% decisions aided); Ethical compliance score/incidents.

Measurement & sources
Pre/post assessments, system logs (decision aid usage), governance records; cohort comparison.

Translation note
Value from reduced Decision Lead Time = time saved × decisions per year × value per decision hour; for strategic decisions translate to avoided loss or faster revenue realisation.

3. TRANSFORM (Organisation Strategy & Transformation)

Redesigns operating models, unlocks structural savings and capability.

Organisation Strategy & Transformation

Jobs we do
Redesign operating models, deliver capability for large transformation, align people/processes, unlock cost savings or revenue capacity.

Primary KPI
Productivity per FTE (or Process Cycle Time for process improvements).

Supporting KPIs
Cost per unit; Error / Rework Rate (%).

Measurement & sources
ERP/process logs, finance data; whole-unit comparison or DID across sites; baseline 12 months.

Translation note
Savings = productivity_gain × FTEs_affected × unit labour cost − transformation cost. Discount savings to NPV.

4. REWARD (Total Rewards Optimisation — TRO)

Design reward mix that maximises perceived employee value and retention.

Total Rewards Optimisation (TRO)

Jobs we do
Maximise perceived employee value per employer £, rebalance spend, model cost vs perceived value, align rewards to EVP and segments.

Primary KPI
Perceived Employee Value per £ Spend (index/£) or Retention Rate for Priority Cohorts (%).

Supporting KPIs
Take-up Rate (%); Cost per Employee (£).

Measurement & sources
Conjoint/preference surveys pre/post, payroll and HRIS for costs and retention. Segment-level analysis.

Translation note
Value = retention_improvement × cohort_size × replacement_cost + value of rebalanced spend. Use TRO cases as exemplars.

5. COMMERCIAL (Sales & Service / KF Sell)

Improves commercial execution, pipeline and seller performance.

Sales & Service (KF Sell)

Win Rate (%) (ppt change).

Supporting KPIs

6. ANALYTICS & GOVERNANCE (People Analytics / KFI Analytics and Value Management / Talent Suite)

Provides insight, predictive models and the governance spine to measure and scale value.

People Analytics / KFI Analytics

Jobs we do
Identify drivers of engagement/performance/turnover, build predictive models, validate assessment-to-outcome links, quantify savings.

Primary KPI
Turnover Risk Predictive Accuracy (AUC) and Realised Turnover Reduction (%).

Supporting KPIs
Feature importance list; Promotion velocity for key talent.

Measurement & sources
HRIS, engagement surveys, backtested model validation. Use holdout tests, cross-validation and post-implementation comparisons.

Translation note
Savings = Turnover_reduction × #employees × replacement_cost.

Value Management / Client Success & Talent Suite

Jobs we do
Align solutions to business outcomes, track adoption and outcomes, govern re-measurement and case capture, deliver Talent Suite components.

Primary KPI
Value Realisation % (Actual vs Target) — governance headline.

Supporting KPIs
Adoption Rate (%); Time to First Value (months).

Measurement & sources
Aggregated from solution KPIs and financial translations; governance cadence with monthly reporting.

Translation note
Value Realisation % = realised_value ÷ target_value; used for scale decisions and case library.

Measurement & implementation rules (standardised across groups)

Baseline window: prefer 12 months. QoH uses 6 months for speed but remains defensible.

Frequency: monthly leading indicators; primary KPI at 6 and 12 months; final financials at 12 months or multi-year horizon for big transformations.

Research design: recommended hierarchy — randomised/staged rollout > matched cohort > difference-in-differences > pre/post with sensitivity analysis. Document attribution fraction and confidence.

Confidence & provenance: every numeric claim must include source excerpt and confidence (green/amber/red). App must surface this.

Translation template: Change × Exposure × Unit value × Realisation rate. Build year-by-year incremental cash flows and discount to NPV. Show arithmetic steps in the report.
