Decided on conversational interface concept
That's a really interesting vision! Here's how a conversation-first, single-page experience could work:

The Concept: "Companion Canvas"
Instead of navigating through Discovery → Alignment → Realization tabs, the user lands on a single conversational interface where the companion becomes the primary way to interact with everything.

How It Would Look
Layout:

Left side (60%): Chat interface with the AI companion - rich messages that can embed cards, forms, KPI widgets, and action buttons inline
Right side (40%): Dynamic context panel that shows whatever the companion is discussing (account details, KPIs, charts, success stories) - updates in real-time as conversation flows
Key Capabilities:

Natural Onboarding

User: "I have a meeting with Nestlé next week about their leadership pipeline"
Companion: Creates account, runs research, shows key insights inline, asks clarifying questions about the theme
Inline Actions

Companion shows KPI recommendations as interactive cards in the chat
User can click to accept, or say "change the target to 15%"
All changes happen through conversation, no form hunting
Proactive Coaching

"Based on your upcoming QBR, here are 3 talking points..."
"I noticed you haven't updated the retention KPI in 30 days - want me to remind you?"
Voice-Ready

Design the interface to work with voice input for hands-free prep during commutes
Technical Approach:

Expand the existing companion tools to cover 100% of app functionality
Stream rich interactive components (not just text) into the chat
The context panel syncs with whatever entity the conversation references
